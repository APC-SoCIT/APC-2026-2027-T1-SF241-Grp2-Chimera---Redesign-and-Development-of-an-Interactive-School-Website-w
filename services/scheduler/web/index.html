Set the school day

Grades 7–8, Grades 9–10, Grade 11 and Grade 12 have separate settings.

Different breaks. Different dismissal times.

Keep recess and lunch separate between groups to avoid canteen crowding. Give each group its own dismissal window. Advisers stay available for their section’s routines, breaks, dismissal and homeroom.

Assign class advisers

Choose the adviser for each section. For a combined class, add another adviser when needed.

Automatic protection during school activities

When school-day settings are complete, advisers cannot teach another class during their section’s morning routines, recess, lunch or dismissal. Homeroom is assigned to an adviser. Supervision is shown separately from teaching minutes.
